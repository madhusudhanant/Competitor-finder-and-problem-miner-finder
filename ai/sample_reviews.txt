The app keeps crashing whenever I try to upload large files. It's really frustrating because I need to share important documents with my team but can't do it reliably.

I love the concept of this service, but the loading times are incredibly slow. Sometimes it takes 30+ seconds just to load my dashboard, which makes it unusable for quick tasks.

The user interface is confusing and not intuitive at all. I spent 20 minutes trying to figure out how to change my profile settings. The navigation menu doesn't make sense.

Customer support is non-existent. I've been waiting for a response to my ticket for over a week now. When you're paying for a service, you expect better support.

The mobile app doesn't sync properly with the web version. I'll make changes on my phone and they don't show up on my computer, which creates confusion and duplicated work.

Battery drain is excessive when using this app. My phone goes from 100% to 20% in just a couple hours of regular usage. This needs to be optimized.

The search functionality is broken. I can't find files that I know exist, and the search results are completely irrelevant to what I'm looking for.

Notifications don't work properly. I'm supposed to get alerts when someone shares a document with me, but I never receive them, causing me to miss important updates.

The pricing is way too expensive for what you get. Compared to competitors, this service offers fewer features at a much higher cost.

Data export is practically impossible. I've been trying to download my data for backup purposes but the export feature either fails or produces corrupted files.
